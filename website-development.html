FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your web project with us.

How long does it take to build a website?+
Timelines vary by project complexity. A simple 5-page business site takes 1–2 weeks. A full custom web application can take 6–12 weeks. We'll give you a precise timeline during our discovery call, and we stick to it with milestone-based delivery.
Do I own the website after it's built?+
Absolutely — 100% ownership. Once the project is delivered and payment is complete, you own all code, design assets, and content. We hand over complete access to all accounts, repositories, and files.
Will my website rank on Google?+
We build every website with technical SEO fundamentals baked in — semantic HTML, fast load times, proper meta structure, sitemaps, and schema markup. For ongoing content and link-building strategy, our Digital Marketing team can help you dominate your search rankings.
Can I update the website myself after launch?+
Yes! We build CMS-powered sites (WordPress, Webflow, Sanity) so you can update text, images, and blog posts without touching code. We also provide a 1-hour training session so you're confident managing your own content from day one.
What if I need changes after the project is done?+
All plans include post-launch support (Starter: 7 days, Professional: 30 days, Enterprise: 90 days). Beyond that, we offer affordable monthly maintenance retainers or ad-hoc change requests billed hourly. You're never left stranded.
Do you work with clients internationally?+
Yes — we work with clients worldwide. Our team communicates asynchronously across time zones, and we schedule calls at mutually convenient times. We've successfully delivered projects for clients in the US, UK, UAE, Australia, and beyond.
